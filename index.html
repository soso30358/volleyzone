THE FUNDAMENTALS

Know the game.

01 / BASICS
THE ESSENTIALS01—04

Simple rules.
Better decisions.

Understand the fundamentals before you step onto the court.

01

Scoring

Rally scoring is used. A team normally needs 25 points and a two-point lead to win a set; the deciding fifth set is usually played to 15.

↗
02

Three touches

A team may use up to three hits to return the ball, not counting a block touch under the standard rules.

↗
03

Rotation

When the receiving team wins the rally and gains the serve, its players rotate one position clockwise.

↗
04

Keep it in play

Send the ball over the net and inside the boundary lines. Avoid catching, holding or carrying the ball.

HOW IT WORKS

Move with purpose.

Players rotate clockwise when their team wins the right to serve from the receiving team.

01
Start in position

Keep the correct rotational order at the moment of service.

02
Win the rally

If you were receiving and win the rally, your team gains the serve.

03
Rotate clockwise

Each player moves to the next numbered position before the next serve.

TIP After the serve, players may move into their tactical positions, following the rules.
